# S59 — The collector thread's birth takes its memory from the manager: notes

The role lines and the reasoning of `PLAN.md` S59, kept here because the
section would otherwise run past forty lines (`dev/INDEX.md`, Conventions).
Deleted with the stage; a ruling or a measurement goes to the journals first.

## The Critic's pass over the draft, 2026-09-18

Nine findings, each with what it changed:

1. **A guard page "through `memory::os`" needs an entry the module has
   not.** `map_aligned` maps read-write and the module declares `mmap` and
   `munmap` alone; glibc adds no guard to a user-provided stack. Without one,
   a frame past the low end of the mapping writes into whatever `mmap` placed
   below it — the next pool region, the next sibling's stack — and nothing
   faults. Accepted: `mprotect` is declared beside the two, the lowest page of
   the one mapping is turned `PROT_NONE`, and the guard is what makes the
   stack figure honest.
2. **The wake word's set and clear were unspecified, and both readings
   failed a case.** Set unconditionally with no clear, every pressure
   collection under forbidden births leaves the elder a stale wake and the
   next born elder runs an unasked round inside
   `a_poll_signals_once_a_registration_filled_a_block_and_not_before`'s
   quiet window; set only while alive, `end_idle_siblings` wakes an `ENDING`
   sibling into nothing and `the_siblings` waits out `PAST_THE_CASE`, and the
   tests' `retire`, which sets `RETIRING` outside any lock, loses its wake to
   a thread between its check and its wait. Accepted: `wake` sets the word
   under the mutex unconditionally and answers `state == ALIVE`;
   `begin_the_thread` clears it before storing `ALIVE`, where the handle was
   published; the stand-in clears it; `retire` sets every slot's word under
   its mutex and then notifies; the consent wait takes the word as the round
   wait does, else it returns at once until its deadline.
3. **`UNBORN` was stored before the runtime exit on the panic path.** The
   drop guard runs before `EXIT_GUARD`'s `ll_thread_exit` on an unwind, so a
   join at the next birth waits on an exit collection, and — speculative,
   unproven — a birth reached from that exit's own frames would join itself.
   Accepted: the trampoline runs `thread_body` under `catch_unwind`, then
   `ll_thread_exit`, then stores `UNBORN`; a join that fails is a refused
   birth, never a reuse of the stack.
4. **`pthread_attr_t` is opaque and platform-sized.** A struct declared too
   small is a silent overwrite of the caller's frame on the pressure path.
   Accepted: a 128-byte, 16-aligned opaque buffer with the real sizes stated
   beside it (56 on glibc and musl, 64 on macOS).
5. **Where an ended sibling's stack goes was unpriced.** Joined only at a
   rebirth that may never come, up to seven mappings and their zombie TCBs
   for the process's life. Accepted the cheaper shape: one stack per slot,
   mapped once and kept across lives, `MAX_COLLECTORS` the bound, so a birth
   after the first makes one join and one create and no `mmap`; a
   `pthread_create` that refuses keeps the stack, notes the refused birth and
   stores `UNBORN`.
6. **The red case had two readings.** Written as "red under a wait that
   sleeps without reading the word first".
7. **The Miri clause could be met by a run that saw nothing.** Every
   `unsafe` line the raw thread adds sits under `cfg(not(miri))`. Accepted:
   the stage's `done:` says the raw thread's own lines have no Miri coverage
   by construction, and the closing record names what covers them.
8. **Smaller omissions.** The name through `pthread_setname_np` on linux;
   `CloseHandle` and an `extern "system"` trampoline on Windows; the probe
   reads the crate's `#[global_allocator]` alone, so libc's own `calloc` of
   the DTV inside `pthread_create` is reported by the create's error code and
   is not the reading; a raw thread's panic prints to the real stderr under
   the harness — cosmetic, not changed.
9. **The stack size was unnamed.** `COLLECTOR_STACK_BYTES`, 2 MiB, std's
   default and not a measured figure: the suite passes under it in the debug
   profile, whose frames are the larger; the trace's worklist is arena-backed
   so no frame scales with the graph. A probe exists — lower the constant
   under the worker suite until it dies — and the record says so rather
   than claiming none.

What the Critic checked and found sound: the join-before-create order (the
state goes `UNBORN` only from the slot's own thread, and a join returns after
the kernel clears the tid, after every TLS destructor); the wake protocol
its callers rely on — false means the poll's flag stands, the answer is
ignored elsewhere — with an unconditional set and the answer read from the
state, and `ENDING` answering false where the handle answered true, an
improvement; `Mutex`/`Condvar` under Miri block the interpreted thread as
`park_timeout` does; no case reads a collector panic through a join; TLS
destructors run on a `pthread_create` thread through `__call_tls_dtors`; the
S59.1 → S59.2 order, separable, with `retire` and `HANDLES` ending with two
arms each.
